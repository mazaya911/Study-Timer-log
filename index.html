⚙️ One quick setup step

This page isn't connected to a Google Sheet yet. In app.js, set APPS_SCRIPT_URL to your deployed Apps Script web app URL:

  1. Make a Google Sheet with header row: Timestamp, Subject, Minutes, Note, Nickname.
  2. Open Extensions > Apps Script, paste in the contents of code.gs, and save.
  3. Click Deploy > New deployment, choose Web app, set Execute as: Me and Who has access: Anyone, then deploy and copy the URL.
  4. Paste that URL as the value of APPS_SCRIPT_URL at the top of app.js, save, and reload this page.
